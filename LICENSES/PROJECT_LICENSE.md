## Research status

Synthetic Human Brain is computational research software. It is a simulation and research framework and is not a claim to reproduce a human brain, human consciousness, or human biological function in its entirety.

The project may model neural structures, dynamics, connectivity, learning, memory, sensory processing, motor control, and interfaces to experimental neural-computing platforms. Such models should be interpreted as engineering and research abstractions.
